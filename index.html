Recruiter Bot

Pick a job to see the best candidates for it, or pick a candidate to see the best jobs for them.

    How is the score calculated?
    • Skills (max 70 points): the share of the job's required skills the candidate has. 2 of 3 skills = 46.7 points.
    • Experience (max 30 points): full points if the candidate meets the job's minimum years. Otherwise it scales down (2 yrs vs a 4 yr minimum = half the points). Extra years above the minimum earn nothing more.
    • Left out: anyone with no required skill in common is not listed.
    • Ties: broken by availability (Immediate first), then more experience.
    • Availability is shown but does not change the score.
